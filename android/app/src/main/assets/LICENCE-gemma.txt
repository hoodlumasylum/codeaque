Smarter Nova — optional on-device model (Gemma 3 1B)
====================================================

CodeAque can download Google's Gemma 3 1B Instruct model (Q4_K_M GGUF)
for use only as Nova's on-device brain. The model is not bundled in the APK.

Licence: Gemma is used under Google's Gemma Terms of Use:
  https://ai.google.dev/gemma/terms

A grown-up must read and accept those terms in Settings before the download
starts. The model stays on this phone and is used only to answer kid-safe
questions as Nova. If the model is not downloaded, the phone is too weak,
or an answer is not safe, Nova falls back to her local answers and (when
Smarter Nova is on) a short Simple English Wikipedia fact (CC BY-SA).

Source (Q4_K_M GGUF):
  https://huggingface.co/bartowski/google_gemma-3-1b-it-GGUF
